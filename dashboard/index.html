    Methodology

    New-hire and senior retention: share of hires still employed 6 (or 12) calendar months after hire; any exit on or before that date counts as not retained. Hires whose window ends after 2025-12-31 are not yet measurable and are counted separately. Senior = Senior Leader.

    Regretted turnover: confirmed regretted exits in the trailing 12 months divided by the average of 13 month-end headcounts.

    Uncertainty: 95% Wilson intervals. A status is met or not met only when the whole interval is on one side of the target, otherwise inconclusive. Verdicts are given per year and for 2021–2025; quarters and months are trends.

    Unverified exits are quarantined from the primary analysis because their termination classification is incomplete; a sensitivity analysis assesses the impact of treating them as valid exits.

    External signals: joined as of the start of each cohort period using conservative fixed publication lags (monthly +2, quarterly +3, annual +7 months). These are approximations based on observed availability, not exact release dates, and today's APIs return revised values.

    Association: Spearman rank correlation within each country (formal, Holm-corrected, 4 tests per objective); pooled and time-adjusted views are descriptive. Bootstrap intervals are exploratory because rows are not independent.
