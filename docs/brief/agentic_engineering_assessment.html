03

Minimum contract

Build one coherent vertical slice

Implementation depth matters more than surface area. Make tradeoffs explicit and keep the core path runnable.

ResearchSource fitness, definitions, licensing, cadence, coverage, and known limitations.
IngestRe-runnable retrieval with raw evidence, failure handling, and source metadata.
CurateCanonical countries, periods, units, dimensions, quality, freshness, and lineage.
AnalyseRetention features, objective status, careful temporal joins, and association analysis.
ExplainInteractive insight experience, limitations, methodology, and decisions.

Public-data minimum

  • At least two authoritative public providers.
  • At least three relevant external indicators.
  • Approximately three years of history where the source supports it.
  • Source URL, licence or terms, cadence, scope, and access date documented.

Source selection

Choose and justify indicators that represent at least two useful lenses, such as labour supply, labour demand, cost-of-living pressure, economic cycle, or workforce structure.

Eurostat, the World Bank, OECD, ILOSTAT, and national statistical offices are examples—not a prescribed answer.

Data layers and contracts

  • Preserve raw source payloads or faithful replay fixtures.
  • Separate source-shaped and canonical records.
  • Expose a consumption-ready analytical product.
  • Retain source, indicator, unit, frequency, period, load time, and quality lineage.

Reliability

  • One documented command for the core workflow.
  • Deterministic reruns and intentional overwrite/upsert behaviour.
  • Useful failure messages and safe partial-failure handling.
  • Tests covering the riskiest assumptions and transformations.

04

One problem, different emphasis

Depth matters, choose where to focus

Data and Software deliver the complete vertical slice. Select where you want to focus more.

Data emphasis

Make the data product dependable

  • Incremental or partition-aware ingestion design.
  • Idempotency, replay, schema contracts, and evolution strategy.
  • Canonical grain, keys, lineage, and reconciliation.
  • Automated quality gates and usable diagnostics.
  • Performance and production-scale reasoning.
Software emphasis

Make the product maintainable and usable

  • Clear adapters, domain boundaries, and configuration.
  • Resilient API handling and observable failures.
  • Packaging, dependency management, and developer experience.
  • Automated tests across service and UI boundaries.
  • Accessible dashboard interaction and graceful empty/error states.

Currently viewing Data emphasis. Reviewers will still expect a working dashboard and clear communication.

Portable assessment stack

Use Python 3.11+ and demonstrate SQL concepts. For local processing, pandas or local PySpark are acceptable. For the experience layer, use Power BI Desktop, or HTML. Avoid paid infrastructure requirements.

Include one production architecture view explaining how your solution would map to ADF-style orchestration, a Databricks or lakehouse environment, and Power BI consumption. The mapping should address secrets, scheduling, observability, storage, access control, and promotion between environments.

One clarification round

You may submit one concise batch of questions early in the exercise. Material answers may be shared with other candidates for consistency. If an answer does not arrive, record a reasonable assumption and proceed.

Timebox honestly

State your approximate effort. If you stop short of a feature, preserve a runnable core and explain the next step. A deliberate cut is stronger evidence than an unfinished broad solution.

06

Agentic work

Use an AI coding agent—and remain accountable

The assessment evaluates how you direct, challenge, and verify agents, not prompt volume or vendor choice.

Show your operating loop

  • How you decomposed the work and controlled scope.
  • Which tasks the agent performed and which decisions remained yours.
  • At least one suggestion you rejected or materially changed.
  • How tests, source evidence, and inspection verified outputs.

Your responsibility

  • Do not submit code or analysis you cannot explain.
  • Do not send sensitive information to an AI service.
  • Do not treat generated citations, schemas, or statistics as facts without checking.
  • Do not hide material failures or manual corrections.
